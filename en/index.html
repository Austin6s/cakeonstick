Good to Know

A few things worth knowing before you order

How do I order and pay?

No online checkout here. Just DM us on Instagram, message us on LINE, or send the form below. We'll confirm the details together, and payment is a simple money transfer once your order is locked in. Nothing to pay up front.

How far in advance should I order?

We take orders every Thursday through Saturday, then make and ship everything the following Wednesday. For custom orders and party packages, reach out 1 to 2 weeks ahead of your event, especially around holidays. For weddings or larger events, feel free to reach out well in advance to book your date. A 30% deposit secures it.

What about shipping and pickup?

We ship cold anywhere in Taiwan for NT$190, free over NT$3,000. Pickup options are still being finalized, so stay tuned.

Can I request a fully custom theme or design?

Custom themes are always welcome. What's possible depends on how well it translates onto a small cakesicle, so just tell us your idea and we'll see what we can do together.
